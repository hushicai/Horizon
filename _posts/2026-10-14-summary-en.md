### [torch-pointcloud: timm-style library for 3D point-cloud models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x4x6ls/i_built_a_pytorch_library_of_pretrained_models/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

torch-pointcloud is a new PyTorch library that unifies multiple 3D point-cloud architectures and their pretrained checkpoints behind a single timm-inspired create\_model\(\) factory. The alpha release supports classification, semantic and part segmentation, detection, and self-supervised pretraining across models including PointNet++, DGCNN, KPConv, RandLA-Net, PointNeXt, PointMLP, Point Transformer v1/v2/v3, Sonata, PointMamba, VoteNet, 3DETR, PointPillars, SECOND, PointRCNN, SPVCNN, OctFormer, and Point-MAE/BERT/GPT. Pretrained weights are hosted on Hugging Face with source attribution \(e.g., .pointcept, .openpoints\). Installation uses pip with PyTorch Geometric wheels for CUDA kernels. All models share a common packed-batch tensor interface and reuse core components such as KNN and FPS rather than re-implementing them.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/arthurdjn · Oct 13, 13:50

**「Background」** The author created torch-pointcloud after encountering installation difficulties with torch-points3d and frustration with fragmented research codebases that require local builds, custom ops, and inconsistent evaluation strategies. Existing libraries often tie models to specific configs and kernels, making reproduction and fair benchmarking difficult. The library adopts the timm pattern—successful in 2D vision—of a single factory function, standardized interfaces, and reusable core layers to address these issues for 3D point-cloud tasks.

**「Impact」** Developers can now pip-install a single package to access pretrained weights from multiple upstream repositories \(Pointcept, OpenPoints, MIT Han Lab, FAIR, OpenMMLab\) and benchmark them under a common evaluation strategy, reducing the friction of reproducing and comparing point-cloud models.
