### [Outlook.com Is Quietly Rejecting Email from Domains Without DMARC](https://mailroute.net/blog/outlook-rejecting-email-without-dmarc) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Outlook.com has started rejecting email from domains that have not implemented DMARC authentication. This policy change affects anyone sending mail to Outlook.com recipients from domains without a DMARC record. Community reports indicate that rejected messages result in 5xx permanent delivery failures, rather than being silently dropped. The enforcement appears to be active as of November 2026, though Microsoft has not issued a formal announcement.

hackernews · xmddmx · Nov 8, 19:20 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50416158)

**「Background: Outlook.s DMARC enforcement」** Since November 5, 2026, Outlook.com and Hotmail have been silently bouncing mail from domains that lack a DMARC record, sending permanent 5xx delivery failures \(550 5.7.1\) to senders. The change is part of Microsoft&\#x27;s broader 2026 cold-email policy, which moved from quarantining low-sender-score mail to rejecting it outright, and is now enforced at the domain level rather than the sender score level. The one-record fix is a DNS TXT record of \`\_dmarc.yourdomain.com\` with \`v=DMARC1; p=reject;\` — once added, Outlook stops rejecting the mail.

The shift matters because many small senders and legacy domains never implemented DMARC, so they were effectively blacklisted by Outlook without knowing it. Community comments note that Outlook has long been an unreliable recipient for self-hosting mail admins, even when SPF, DKIM, and DMARC were all properly configured; the new policy simply makes the gap visible.

**「Impact」** The immediate consequence is that any domain without a DMARC policy will have its emails rejected by Outlook.com, preventing messages from reaching recipients using that mailbox provider. Domain owners and email administrators must implement DMARC authentication to restore deliverability, making this an urgent operational concern for organizations that have not yet adopted email authentication standards.

**「Community Discussion」** Community commentators expressed a range of views: some welcomed the move as long overdue, while others criticized Outlook.com for its opaque blocking practices and for dropping emails even from domains with properly configured SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. One contributor clarified that rejections are not silent but result in 5xx permanent delivery failures, contradicting the characterization of the enforcement as quiet.

### [Reddit user proposes diffusion models to generate neural network weights](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xqsl8d/what_if_diffusion_models_generates_neural_network/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Reddit user has proposed using diffusion models to generate neural network weights from text prompts instead of images, sharing a small experimental proof-of-concept on GitHub \(github.com/vagrillo/nngen\) and a test webapp. The implementation is only partially trained—about 3,000 of 4,000 steps completed on a laptop CPU—and the author has not found a similar published paper, asking the community for related work and contributions. The results remain preliminary, with the author describing them as the best outcomes achieved so far. No shipped product or independently verified capability is described.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Specific-Tax-6700 · Nov 8, 07:20

**「Background」** Diffusion models are well-established for generating images from text by learning luminance distributions across red, green, and blue channel matrices. A line of prior work has adapted this framework to synthesize task-specific neural network weights from text prompts, providing the technical context for the proposed approach.

**「Impact on AI/ML」** The post proposes a speculative but concrete proof-of-concept using diffusion models to generate neural network weights from text prompts, demonstrated with a small GitHub project and preliminary results. Independent work has already explored similar directions: NNiT \(arXiv 2603.00180\) addresses weight-agnostic neural network generation via diffusion models, while alphaXiv \(2605.18632\) demonstrates efficient neural architecture search where models like GHN-3 predict performant weights for complex architectures in a single forward pass. OpenReview also hosts a Diffusion-based Neural Network Weights Generation paper. The concept remains experimental, but the existence of prior research indicates the direction is being actively explored rather than purely speculative.

**「AI moats: model layer versus product layer」** In AI industry discussions, a &quot;moat&quot; means a durable competitive advantage that shields a business from competitors. A central unresolved debate is whether that advantage lies at the model layer—better foundation models and benchmark scores—or at the product layer, where user context, proprietary data, distribution, and trust make a service difficult to copy. Yao Shunyu&\#x27;s remarks enter that debate from Tencent&\#x27;s position as operator of WeChat, a consumer platform with extensive user relationships and data.

**「Impact」** This perspective challenges the prevailing assumption that model capability is the primary competitive moat in AI, suggesting that companies focusing solely on model performance may be building less defensible positions than those integrating AI into products with user context and proprietary data.
