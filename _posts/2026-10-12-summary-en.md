**「Impact」** Users relying on passkeys tied to a single device vendor may face limited portability, as attestation can only be required at registration and not retroactively, and browser permission prompts for attestation can interrupt registration flows if declined, potentially locking users into specific authenticator ecosystems.

### [Palette Atlas adds sliced Wasserstein + HNSW for sub‑millisecond color‑scheme search](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x39uu6/sliced_wasserstein_embeddings_hnsw_for/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Palette Atlas now uses sliced Wasserstein embeddings combined with a custom HNSW index to enable fast approximate nearest‑neighbor search over its 48,695 painting color schemes. The method projects each 128‑color palette onto 8 Fibonacci‑hemisphere directions, computes 16 quantiles, and uses L1 distance as a lower bound on Earth Mover’s Distance, achieving 1.000 recall@10 at 0.25 ms per query—over 50× faster than brute‑force EMD. Compared to exact EMD, the sliced 8×16 L1 approach reaches 78 % overlap with the exact top‑10 results and keeps them only 1.8 % farther on average.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Potential-Barber8658 · Oct 11, 14:39

**「Background」** Color‑scheme search traditionally relies on Earth Mover’s Distance \(EMD\) computed on histogram data, which is accurate but too slow for large collections. Sliced Wasserstein embeddings provide a fast lower‑bound approximation by projecting histograms onto random directions and computing 1‑D Wasserstein distances. Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs \(HNSW\) enable sub‑millisecond approximate nearest‑neighbor queries even in low‑dimensional spaces.

**「Impact」** Users of Palette Atlas can now retrieve color‑matching paintings in under a millisecond with exact top‑10 recall, a &gt;50× performance gain over the previous EMD‑based search.
