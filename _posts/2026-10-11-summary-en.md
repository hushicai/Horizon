### [Google and Meta activate Iraq fiber backups amid Red Sea cable threats](https://restofworld.org/2026/google-meta-red-sea-subsea-cables-houthi-yemen/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Escalating conflict near the Bab el-Mandab Strait now threatens over 90% of the submarine cables linking Europe and Asia, prompting Google and Meta to begin routing actual traffic through newly activated land‑based fiber routes in Iraq. Google acquired two fiber lines along Turkey’s national pipeline in September, paying about $7 million—roughly 2–3 times the expected cost of new lines in Turkey—and both companies have started using the Iraqi land routes as backups. Microsoft has announced plans to invest more than $400 million by 2030 in Middle East subsea and land connectivity, reflecting broader industry efforts to safeguard global internet bandwidth.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 10, 08:00

**「Background」** Submarine cables form the cheapest and primary backbone for trans‑continental internet traffic, but they are vulnerable to geopolitical disruptions such as the Red Sea conflict. Land‑based fiber routes are typically used only as emergency backups because they are more expensive and have higher latency. The recent escalation near the Bab el-Mandab Strait directly endangers the majority of Europe‑Asia cable capacity, motivating tech firms to accelerate deployment of alternative terrestrial pathways.

**「Impact」** By shifting some traffic to the Iraqi land‑based fiber, Google and Meta reduce their exposure to Red Sea cable outages, improving the reliability of Euro‑Asian internet connectivity for AI, ML, and cloud services that depend on stable bandwidth.

### [Reddit User Seeks Hardware Advice After Qwen3.5-14B Fine-Tuning Failure](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x29lqj/help_with_choosing_hardware_p/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

In a post published Oct. 10, 2026, a Reddit practitioner asks the machine learning community for hardware recommendations after encountering out-of-memory errors while full fine-tuning \`Qwen3.5-14B-Base\`. The failed instance provided 4x12GB GPUs and 48GB of system RAM, which proved insufficient for a dataset of approximately 18,000 long examples that required full fine-tuning rather than parameter-efficient methods like LoRA. The user reported spending roughly $3 adjusting training settings before concluding that the hardware capacity itself was the bottleneck.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/minedroid1 · Oct 10, 08:00

**「Hardware context for fine‑tuning Qwen3.5‑14B」** The user is trying to fine‑tune the Qwen3.5‑14B‑Base model, a 14‑billion‑parameter open‑weight large language model developed by Alibaba Cloud \(see Qwen description\). Full fine‑tuning of a model this size requires GPU memory far exceeding the 12 GB per card available on the rented vast.ai instance, because the model weights alone occupy roughly 28 GB in FP16 and additional memory is needed for optimizer states, gradients, and activations. Consequently, the training repeatedly ran out of memory, prompting the request for hardware recommendations that can accommodate the model’s memory footprint.

**「Impact」** Users planning similar full fine-tunes should treat 4x12GB GPU configurations paired with 48GB of system RAM as a risk, since this specific setup failed during training on a 14-billion-parameter model with long, divergent examples.

### [Claude Dynamic Multi-Agent Workflows Enter Public Testing](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2108591328732856655) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Claude has launched public testing of its Managed Agents Dynamic Workflows, a new multi-agent orchestration feature that enables a primary agent to plan and coordinate parallel sub-agents across execution phases. The system is designed for large-scale tasks that exceed the capacity of a single conversation, such as reviewing hundreds of documents. Workflows run on servers in the background with a default 24-hour time limit, and their progress is tracked via an event stream. Sub-agents can execute in parallel within each phase, with results passed between stages and ultimately aggregated by the main agent.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 10, 08:30

**「Background」** The feature addresses a limitation of standard conversational AI, where a single model response or conversation thread cannot effectively handle complex, multi-stage tasks at scale. Claude&\#x27;s Managed Agents platform previously offered simpler agent capabilities, and Dynamic Workflows extends that model by introducing explicit phase-based planning, parallel sub-agent execution, and result aggregation. This reflects a broader industry shift toward orchestrated multi-agent systems that decompose large problems into coordinated subtasks.

**「Impact」** Developers building on Claude can now tackle larger, more complex workflows—such as bulk document review or multi-step research pipelines—without needing to manually orchestrate separate agent calls. The 24-hour background runtime and event-stream status tracking mean tasks can proceed asynchronously, though developers must account for phase dependencies and result-passing semantics when designing workflows. As this is a public testing release, behavior, limits, and the programming model may evolve before general availability.
